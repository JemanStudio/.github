# Security policy

If you believe you have found a security or privacy issue in a Jeman app or on jeman.net, please report it privately.

## How to report

Email [support@jeman.net](mailto:support@jeman.net) with the subject line **Security**. Please include:

- The app and version (or the jeman.net page) affected
- Your macOS or iOS version
- Steps to reproduce, and what an attacker could do with it

Please don't open a public issue for security reports, and never send passwords, tokens or other credentials.

## What happens next

A real person will reply, usually within 2 business days, and let you know how we plan to fix it.

## Scope

- Netlink for Mac
- Nekot for iPhone
- jeman.net
